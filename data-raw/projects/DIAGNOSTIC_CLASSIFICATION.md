# Diagnostic calculation boundary

The source is `../VPRO_ACCESS/VPro64_forAI/Modules/V7mdlDiagnostic.txt` and the active caller is `Modules/V7mdlReportsShortVeg.txt`.

`V7mdlReportsShortVeg.CreateDiagnostic` (2366–2445) copies break-code plot memberships into `USysSuTableDynamic_SU`, calls `V7mdlDiagnostic.MakeDiagnosticData("SuTable")`, and creates `MyRS5` separately for each `USysBreakTable.Reference` (2401–2416). `MakeDiagnosticData` (193–265) unpivots non-null Cover1–7, sums cover by plot/species (optionally through `LumpVegData`), counts SU plots, then aggregates per reference/unit/species into `MyRS4`. `MyRS5` pivots those results by SiteUnit, yielding one row per species and cells `DiagnosticPresenceClass(nCover / FirstOfnPlots) & ' - ' & SignifClass(TotalCover / denominator)`. The denominator is `FirstOfnPlots` if `AverageType = 1`, otherwise `nCover`. The presence helper (275–301) emits 1–5 for values from zero through one; `SignifClass` in `V7mdlValueConversions` emits `+` or 1–9 for its valid interval. The crosstab has no explicit `PIVOT ... IN` ordering: callers must retain their observed field order rather than infer site-unit order. The report caller skips crosstabs with fewer than three fields (species plus at least two units).

`vpro_diagnostic_classify()` translates **only** `V7mdlDiagnostic.Diagnostic` lines 91–169 for one row of already-formatted, ordered unit cells. It selects the first field with the maximum presence, interprets the final `+` as numeric zero (VBA `Val("+")`), counts near-maximum presence/significance with strict `>`, and assembles categories in source order. It returns missing unit/diagnosis when Access would write no row. For valid presence classes 1–5, `ic` is unreachable: with maximum 2, every other non-null class is 1 or 2 and therefore counts as near-maximum; with maximum 3 or greater, unique near-maximum sets `d`. The `d, cd` and `d, c` combinations are possible and retained.

Access emitted seven rows and omitted three; `vpro_diagnostic_classify()` agreed on unit and diagnosis for **all ten** cases. The package test fixes those observed results independently of the VM.

This probe bypassed `CreateDiagnostic`, `MakeDiagnosticData`, `MyRS4`, and the report UI. It establishes the classification and no-row behavior only for the recorded, well-formed `MyRS5` cells, not real-world crosstab ordering, report values, invalid VBA coercion, or full end-to-end equivalence.

Intentional limits: character inputs must have distinct nonempty names and exact `"1 - +"` through `"5 - 9"` formatting; malformed or out-of-range codes fail instead of allowing `Val()` to silently interpret their first/last characters. Missing cells are skipped. No SQL, Access recordsets, `Diagnostic`/`DiagnosticGroup` table writes, report integration, unspecified crosstab pivot ordering, or stale-query behavior is translated. The Access `Diagnostic` procedure must remain unresolved in parity inventory until the surrounding workflow is implemented and reviewed; this isolated calculation is not proof of complete procedure equivalence.
