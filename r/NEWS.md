# vpro 2.0.0

* **Workspace, Standards & Plot Extraction:** Connect directly to your live VPro Desktop project or bundled `Sample` project without file-locking conflicts (`vpro_connect()`, `vpro_use()`, `vpro_status()`), verify 2026 `BECdb v13` and `BCPlantTaxonomy v19` standards (`vpro_standards()`), convert legacy Access `.accdb`/`.mdb` databases (`vpro_import_access()`), and extract plot environment, lumped vegetation (`0` to under `100%` cover with `99%` per-layer caps), and `LMH25 (2023)`-ordered soil horizons while preserving leading zeroes on `PlotNumber` (`vpro_env()`, `vpro_veg()`, `vpro_soil()`, `vpro_hierarchy()`).
* **Quality Checks, Community Clustering & Spatial Export:** Validate field plots against `LMH25 (2023)` soil, substrate, and code rules (`vpro_validate()`), build row-aligned plot-by-species community matrices (`vpro_matrix()`), run fuzzy noise, hierarchical, or PAM clustering with indicator species analysis (`vpro_cluster()`, `vpro_indicators()`), save candidate Site Unit or lumping tables safely back to VPro Desktop (`vpro_write_su()`, `vpro_write_lump()`), and export spatial layers to GeoPackage (`vpro_sf()`, `vpro_export_gpkg()`).
* **BEC Diagnostic Synthesis & Reports:** Compute BEC differential and constancy classes, pairwise unit similarity, misplaced-plot outlier queues, set-algebra plot profiling, and hierarchy breakpoint summaries, and export formatted Excel workbooks (`vpro_diagnostics()`, `vpro_pairwise()`, `vpro_outliers()`, `vpro_profile()`, `vpro_summary_veg()`, `vpro_summary_env()`, `vpro_export_xlsx()`).
* **Climate Normals, Ministry aSMR, Edatopic Grids & Quarto Guides:** Downscale plot climate normals (sharing cached results with VPro Desktop and falling back offline to BGC lapse models), evaluate Ministry `aSMR` water-balance scores, plot dual relative and actual `9 x 5` edatopic grids, and render B.C. Government-styled Quarto HTML or PDF field guides (`vpro_climate()`, `vpro_asmr()`, `vpro_edatopic_grid()`, `vpro_plot_edatope()`, `vpro_render_guide()`).
* **Cloud `BECMaster` Queries & Regional Checkouts:** Query the provincial `BECMaster` repository directly from R with automatic sensitive-species coordinate redaction for non-custodians (`vpro_master_connect()`, `vpro_master_env()`, `vpro_master_veg()`, `vpro_master_soil()`, `vpro_master_workspace()`), synchronize local `VLists.db` standards (`vpro_master_standards_sync()`), verify tamper-evident audit logs (`vpro_master_audit()`), and check out regional working subsets (`vpro_master_pull()`).
* **Contributor Staging, Custodian Review & Portable `.rds` Bundles:** Submit validated regional edits to staging (`vpro_staging_submit()`), review 2-way or 3-way diffs with protected provincial fields (`BECSiteUnit`, `BEC_Use`, `GIS_BGC`), approve or reject merges (`vpro_staging_diff()`, `vpro_staging_merge()`, `vpro_staging_reject()`), publish multi-format releases (`vpro_master_publish_snapshot()`), and archive self-contained `.rds` project bundles for offline reproducible analysis (`vpro_export_rds()`, `vpro_import_rds()`).
